About Dr. Abiola Arogundade

Empowering Youth, Transforming Nigeria

Dr. Abiola Arogundade serves as the Senior Special Assistant to President Bola Ahmed Tinubu on Technical, Vocational & Entrepreneurship Education (SSAP-TVEE). She holds a Professional Doctorate in Business Management with specialization in Business Economics and Finance from City University of Paris.

Her office spearheads Federal Government initiatives to promote technical, vocational, and entrepreneurial skills development as a cornerstone of the "Renewed Hope Agenda" for economic growth and job creation in Nigeria.
