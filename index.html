About Dr. Abiola Arogundade

Senior Special Assistant to the President (SSAP-TVEE)

Dr. Abiola Arogundade serves as the Senior Special Assistant to President Bola Ahmed Tinubu on Technical, Vocational & Entrepreneurship Education. She holds a Professional Doctorate in Business Management with specialization in Business Economics and Finance from City University of Paris.

Her office drives the Federal Government's initiatives to promote technical, vocational, and entrepreneurial skills development as a key pillar of the "Renewed Hope Agenda" for economic growth and job creation in Nigeria.

Frequently Asked Questions

The Senior Special Assistant to the President on Technical, Vocational & Entrepreneurship Education (SSAP-TVEE) drives Federal Government initiatives to promote skills development, job creation, and entrepreneurship as key pillars of the Renewed Hope Agenda.

Training programs are announced through our official channels. Interested participants can register through designated platforms when programs are launched. Requirements vary by program but typically target Nigerian youth aged 18-35.

Programs cover diverse areas including shoemaking, leather crafting, modern technical skills, AI and robotics, entrepreneurship, and various vocational trades. Training includes both technical skills and business management.

Yes! Participants receive comprehensive support including startup grants, business starter kits, mentorship programs, and assistance with job placement or business establishment.

The Unlock Campaign is a nationwide initiative to identify skilled Nigerians across various trades. Participants are assessed and provided with grants, equipment, and mentorship to scale their businesses and create employment.

Organizations interested in partnering can reach out through our official contact channels. We welcome collaborations with private sector, educational institutions, and NGOs committed to youth empowerment and skills development.
