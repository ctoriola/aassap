### **3. Accessibility Improvements** ✅
**Goal:** WCAG 2.1 AA compliance through better alt text and ARIA labels

#### Alt Text Enhanced (25+ Images):

**Homepage:**
- ✅ Banner images: Descriptive of TVEE programs and youth training
- ✅ Statistics icons: Explains what each icon represents
- ✅ Program images: Context-specific descriptions
- ✅ Team/participant photos: Identifies activities and context

**About Page:**
- ✅ Banner: "Dr. Abiola Arogundade at TVEE program event"
- ✅ Activity photos: "TVEE skills training session with participants"
- ✅ Impact photos: "Beneficiaries of TVEE entrepreneurship programs"
- ✅ Logo: "SSAP-TVEE Office Logo"

#### ARIA Labels Added:

**Interactive Elements:**
```html
✅ Primary CTA: aria-label="Contact SSAP-TVEE office"
✅ Scroll button: aria-label="Scroll to impact statistics"
✅ Email input: aria-label="Email address for TVEE updates"
✅ Subscribe button: aria-label="Subscribe to TVEE newsletter"
✅ Navigation links: aria-label for clarity
```

**Impact:**
- ✅ Screen readers can now properly describe all content
- ✅ Images convey meaning, not just decoration
- ✅ Interactive elements have clear purpose statements
- ✅ Better SEO through descriptive alt text
